What the house holds

A gathering space,
written as a house.

XI is built for people who already move through New York and the East End — operators, brokers, and the rooms they inhabit. Not a public club. Not a ticketed series.

The work of the house is simple: set a table, decide who sits, and keep the list small enough that an evening still feels like one room. What follows is how that work is offered.

The house

A room for people who already know each other — or should

A private gathering, not a networking floor. Built for connectors across business, culture, media, and the trades that actually close in this city. The room is the product.

The table

Private dining

Long dinners in New York and house suppers on the East End. Courses as conversation. No tasting-menu theater for strangers. If the table is full, it is full.

The season

Evenings, not a calendar product

A year of named nights: dinners, house evenings, talk, water. Season 2026 is assembled by hand. Dates attach after the room is set. There is no public inventory.

Introductions

Who sits is the architecture

We make introductions at the table, not in a corridor with badges. If you are already useful to the people in these rooms, the house is how you sit down without performing it.

Two rooms

New York and the Hamptons

One list, two addresses. The city in season and the East End when the light goes long. We do not tour. We do not open a third door to prove we can.

The list

Inquiry, not an application portal

Write if the house already sounds like home. We answer by hand. There is no public membership checkout, and no fee on this page.

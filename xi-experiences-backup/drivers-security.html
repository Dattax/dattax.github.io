Drivers &
Security

XI Experiences offers professional driver and security services tailored to each client’s individual needs.

Professional Drivers

Private driver services are available for short-term, full-day, recurring, and ongoing transportation needs. Vehicle arrangements can also be coordinated when needed.

Armed Driver & Security

For clients requiring an additional level of protection, armed driver-security professionals can be arranged, combining private transportation with personal security.

Armed Security Personnel

Professional armed security personnel are available for private clients, retail locations, events, and other assignments requiring dedicated security coverage.

Event & Pop-Up Security

Security teams can be coordinated for private events, pop-ups, activations, and larger gatherings, with the ability to scale staffing based on the size and needs of the event.

Recurring & Ongoing Coverage

Driver and security services can be arranged on a recurring or ongoing basis, with customized service options available for clients requiring consistent coverage.

Last-Minute & Special Requests

Evening, weekend, holiday, and last-minute requests can be accommodated based on availability.

Customized Service

Each assignment is tailored based on scheduling, location, duration, transportation needs, staffing requirements, and level of security requested.

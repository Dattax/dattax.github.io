About

XI draws from soccer's iconic starting XI—the eleven players chosen to represent their team. It's a symbol of trust, exclusivity, and excellence. Being selected for the Starting XI means you've earned your place among the best.

At XI Experiences, we don't simply coordinate events. We create moments through relationships, access, and exceptional hospitality.

Bio

Shaun Moamem is the Founder of XI Experiences, a luxury events and experiences company creating curated gatherings in New York City, the Hamptons, Miami, and select destinations. From intimate dinners to large-scale productions and destination retreats, Shaun designs experiences that feel both exclusive and effortless — and he builds them with the wonderful team around him.

With a career built on relationships and execution, Shaun brings an extensive network. Prior to XI, he spent over 2 decades in the New York real estate and hospitality industries. There, he specialized in creating strategic partnerships, curating exclusive events, and bringing together influential people and brands across both industries.

Throughout his career, Shaun has cultivated a reputation for understanding client needs and executing complex projects with attention and discretion. With XI Experiences, he brings those strengths into events and hospitality—creating experiences centered around exceptional settings, thoughtful details, and genuine connection.

His vision: to create experiences people don't just attend, but remember.

About the house

XI is a number
we keep small.

Eleven is the first eleven — the ones who start. The room has thinned. The people who remain are why the table was set.

XI holds private events in New York and the Hamptons. It is not a club with a card, and it is not a ticketed series. Two addresses. One list.

How we keep it

Principles,
before anything else.

  1. Invitation, never a ticket

    No inventory. No price on the door. If the evening is right, we write.

  2. Two rooms, not a circuit

    The city and the East End. We do not tour. We do not franchise the table.

  3. The list is the room

    Who sits is the architecture. Introductions happen at the table.

  4. Return is the proof

    We do not count heads. We notice who comes back.

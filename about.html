Bio

Shaun Moamem is the Founder of XI Premier Productions, a luxury events and experiences company creating curated gatherings in New York City, the Hamptons, and select destinations. From intimate dinners to large-scale productions and destination retreats, Shaun designs experiences that feel both exclusive and effortless.

With a career built on relationships and execution, Shaun brings an extensive network across real estate, finance, hospitality, and entertainment. Prior to XI, he spent over a decade in New York real estate, beginning at Plaza Real Estate Group before joining RKF (Robert K. Futterman & Associates), a premier commercial brokerage later acquired by Newmark Group. There, he specialized in retail leasing and investment sales for high-profile Manhattan transactions.

Throughout his career, Shaun has cultivated a reputation for understanding client needs and executing complex projects with attention and discretion. With XI Premier Productions, he brings those strengths into events and hospitality—creating experiences centered around exceptional settings, thoughtful details, and genuine connection.

His vision: to create experiences people don't just attend, but remember.

About

A steady presence for a complicated season.

MEd in Counseling, The Pennsylvania State University

I offer counseling for adults and couples who want a calm, confidential place to sort through what is heavy — anxiety, life transitions, relationships, and the quieter work of becoming more themselves.

Clients often come to me when something that used to work no longer does — a relationship, a job, a way of holding it all together. You do not need a crisis to begin. If you are looking for a thoughtful partner in the conversation, I would be glad to hear from you.

Approach

Curious, collaborative, and paced with care.

  • You set the agenda.

    I practice in a person-centered way. We start with what is most alive for you, and we move at a pace that feels workable — not rushed, and not so cautious that nothing changes.

  • Integrative, not one-size-fits-all.

    My work is informed by attachment theory and cognitive-behavioral tools, used lightly and only when they help. I am less interested in a method than in a relationship that can hold the truth of your life.

  • The room is yours.

    Sessions are confidential, unhurried, and free of jargon. We can meet in person at my Brooklyn office or by a secure telehealth video session, whichever lets you show up more fully.

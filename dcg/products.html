FRC4U

Coordinates social & health services

An FRC, or Family Resource Center, often operates in partnership with local School Districts and serves as an important gateway for providing needed services to school children and their family members. This model is serving well in the USA.

For several years, DCG's software system has helped care providers efficiently deliver social and health care services and coordinate those services seamlessly, round the clock (for example, NSD/NCCFRC in National City, CA). It also supports and strengthens the human network that connects members of social- and health-care organizations.

  • Help is available from Day Care to Home Care
  • Work closely with the customers to their satisfaction
  • Connect with the community for better help

Choice4Fitness

For any health conscious individual

This system provides tools that any health conscious individual, whether a healthy person or a patient, can use to monitor their fitness level.

Its purpose is to give users, particularly those who are overweight or obese and suffering from related complications such as cardiovascular diseases and diabetes, the tools and educational information needed to make smart decisions to remain healthy.

  • Synchronise with Bluetooth enabled medical devices
  • Mobile apps for connectivity while on-the-go
  • Educational tool for updated information on nutrition and physical activities
  • Upload laboratory test results
  • Generate reports for monitoring

CHOIS

Childhood Obesity Informatics System

CHOIS, or Childhood Obesity Informatics System, was developed to provide health-IT support for screening and monitoring the health of school children.

Its web application provides web-based forms with 112 different fields, ranging from demographic data and height and weight for BMI to genomic information. Automatic computation of BMI, BMI percentile and an obesity risk alert are built in, and the portal provides updated information on how to control overweight and obesity.

  • Mobile apps for connectivity at the point-of-care
  • Educational tool for updated information on obesity control
  • Upload laboratory test results
  • Generate reports for monitoring

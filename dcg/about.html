[A] About

About the founding president and this consulting group

Datta Consulting Group primarily serves organizations in social- and healthcare, with a focus on biomedical informatics.

Dr. Datta's personal site

[01] Founder

Dr. Arun K. Datta

The company became a reality because of Dr. Datta's students of MS Computer Science. He taught more than 1000 students who took computer science courses with him. Among these students, a significant number worked on his funded projects, primarily involving Biomedical Informatics.

In 2015, this academician turned into an entrepreneur and formed this consulting group with the support of his past students and staff members, besides new ones.

Besides R&D activities in Biomedical Informatics, the group provides software products (see Products) and supporting services to organizations primarily serving social- and healthcare.

Previously, as Director of the National University Community Research Institute (NUCRI) at National University, his role was to secure projects and work in cooperation with universities, research institutes and community organizations providing social- and healthcare services. He also teaches technology-related courses.

[02] Mission & vision

Appropriate information helps developing knowledge that helps taking smart decisions.

MISSION

Besides developing custom software, DCG's mission is to provide relevant information on biomedical science that can help a user to take smart decisions on his or her fitness level.

VISION

DCG's vision is to be a significant partner in this field of biomedical informatics.

[03] Expertise

What the group brings

  • Expertise ranges from database development to data analytics for knowledge discovery (data mining)
  • Consultancy particularly on Biomedical Informatics (Health Informatics, Bioinformatics) for Precision Medicine
  • R&D reports for pharmaceutical and biotech companies, as a valuable partner
  • Working closely with customers to their satisfaction
  • Education and Training

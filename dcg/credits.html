Photograph credits

This folder holds photographs used on the Datta Consulting Group draft site.

Original Datta Consulting Group images from the current website were reused

where they were authentic and of usable quality. Additional photographs were

selected from a candidate set sourced on 26 November 2026 (America/New_York)

from Wikimedia Commons (CC0, public domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA) and Pexels

(Pexels License).

All third-party files were resized and saved as WebP plus JPEG, each under

about 300 KB, with a maximum width of about 1,600 px. Crops and resizes are

noted below. CSS color overlays (navy/teal) are applied in the browser and

are not baked into these files.

Please keep this file (or an equivalent credits page) with the site so CC BY

and CC BY-SA terms are met. Pexels License photographs may be used without

attribution; credits are included here for completeness.

Brand mark (concept 15, “Cube in D”)

Deep selected this concept, made by Dev Buddy, for use on the draft site for now. Source files live in assets/img/brand/ (SVG plus @2x PNG; text is outlined). The header uses the color lockup; navy surfaces such as the footer use a reversed lockup without the official navy plate so it sits on the footer colour. Favicons and the Apple touch icon are generated from the icon. og-image.png and the Organization structured-data logo (logo.png) use the new lockup. Earlier marks (the original live-site logo, and concept 9 “Echo D”) are in assets/img/archive/.

Current-site images that were not reused, and why:

  • Homepage banners 1–4 (DCG banner 1.jpg–4.jpg): dated composites with marketing copy baked into the image, or cheesy stock (thumbs-up, chess piece, traveller and skyscrapers).
  • Banner 5 (lab / genomics collage): the right *theme* (precision medicine) is kept via a higher-quality laboratory photograph; the original is a low-quality composite with baked-in text.
  • frcimage.jpg / frc.jpg: generic smiling crowd / icon collage; would look like a fake “our team” photo.
  • fitnessHir.jpg / fitness.jpg: dated stock of a woman with a bathroom scale on a beach.
  • choisHir.jpg / chois.jpg: cropped midsection stock used to illustrate obesity; not appropriate.
  • akdpic2.jpg: a real photo of Dr. Datta teaching, but it includes identifiable students and a dated flowchart overlay, so it was not used.

Precision Medicine Support

We provide services that support delivery of quality healthcare at the individualized level for Precision Medicine.

This includes Genomic Analysis, and analysis of laboratory test results coupled with health data and demographic information captured in EHR (electronic health records) and PHR (personal health records).

DCG's capability statement notes that the proverb "same size does not fit all" applies even to treating a patient, particularly for patients with chronic diseases such as cancer, where factors including a patient's genomic profile need to be considered.

  • Genomic analysis
  • Laboratory test results analysis
  • Health data and demographic information from EHR and PHR
